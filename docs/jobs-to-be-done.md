# Jobs To Be Done

**J-1 (Trip Treasurer):** When a group member pays for a shared expense during
a trip where people join or leave along the way, I want to note what was spent,
who paid, and which members were sharing that particular cost, so I have a
complete account of every transaction by the time the trip ends.

**J-2 (Trip Treasurer):** When expenses are split among different subsets of
the group — some covering the full party, others only the people present for
that leg — I want every person's total to reflect only the costs they were
actually part of, so nobody pays for a stretch of the trip they weren't on and
nobody subsidises expenses that weren't theirs.

**J-3 (Trip Treasurer):** When I know everyone's net position, I want to work
out a set of transfers that clears all the debts, so the group can settle up
without anyone having to chase payments from multiple people or make more
transactions than necessary.

**J-4 (Trip Treasurer):** When a group member challenges a figure or says the
split doesn't seem right, I want to show exactly which expenses contributed to
their total, so the group accepts the result without argument and no one has to
take my word for it.
